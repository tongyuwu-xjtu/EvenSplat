Abstract

Multi-view reconstruction under changing exposure and spatially uneven illumination often entangles capture-specific lighting with scene appearance. EvenSplat addresses this problem by coupling an image-space illumination decomposition with a Gaussian-level illumination field, while camera-response correction and local exposure compensation absorb image-specific residuals. The method targets three challenging settings—cross-view exposure variation (CEV), spatial illumination variation (SIV), and high-contrast illumination (HCI)—and consistently improves illumination-robust novel-view synthesis, with particularly strong gains under high-contrast lighting.

Method

EvenSplat jointly optimizes an image-space decomposition branch and a Gaussian-level illumination field. Illumination alignment couples the 2D and 3D representations, while CRN and ILEC absorb global and spatial image-specific photometric residuals during training.
